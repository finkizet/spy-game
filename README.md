# Spy Game

A party game for 4–10 players.

**[Play here](https://finkizet.github.io/spy-game/)**

## How to play

Players join a lobby and choose a theme. One player is secretly assigned the role of **spy**, they don't know what card or character was chosen, and nobody knows who the spy is.

Players take turns saying words or hints related to the theme, but without being too obvious. The spy tries to blend in by figuring out the answer from context, while the other players try to identify who the spy is.
